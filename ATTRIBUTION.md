Every dataset in this repo's starters belongs to the agency that publishes it. Their terms apply to anything you build. This page is a summary to help you follow them, not legal advice, and the agencies can change their terms at any time. When in doubt, read the source.

## How to say it

Built *on* their data, never *with* them. "A bus tracker built on VIA's open data" is accurate. "A bus tracker with VIA" or "in partnership with the City" implies an agreement that doesn't exist, and VIA's license explicitly forbids it.
