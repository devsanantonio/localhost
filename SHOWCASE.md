# Showcase

Things people have built with San Antonio's open data. Add yours with a pull request: one line, newest at the top.

Format: `- [Project name](link) — what it does. Sources: City / County / VIA. By @your-github`

- [Outrun the 4](https://www.devsa.community/localhost#via) — a Dino Run-style race up San Pedro against the next real Route 4, moving on VIA's published schedule; when it catches you, the next real departures. ([code](https://github.com/devsanantonio/next-devsa/blob/main/components/localhost/outrun-the-4.tsx)) Sources: VIA. By @devsanantonio
- [Watch downtown get built](https://www.devsa.community/localhost#city) — every building permit issued downtown since January 2025, lit in the order it was issued on the City's street map; click a spot for a live query. ([code](https://github.com/devsanantonio/next-devsa/blob/main/components/localhost/watch-it-get-built.tsx)) Sources: City. By @devsanantonio
- [Follow the water](https://www.devsa.community/localhost#bexar-county) — San Antonio from 1718, scrolled: the acequias appear in the year they were dug and go dry in the year they were abandoned, under the 1896 city, ending at Geekdom's corner. ([code](https://github.com/devsanantonio/next-devsa/blob/main/components/localhost/follow-the-water.tsx)) Sources: County. By @devsanantonio
